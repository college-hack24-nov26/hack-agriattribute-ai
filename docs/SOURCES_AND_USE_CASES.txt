AGRIATTRIBUTE AI - SOURCES USED AND WHY
Team HA097 · COEP Phaltan Hackathon 2K26 · AgriTech: Yield Attribution and ROI prediction
=====================================================================================

How to read the STATUS column
  LIVE        fetched from the provider while the app runs
  SNAPSHOT    real reference data, bundled as a file or cached copy (not live)
  REFERENCE   official values typed into the code from a published standard
  SIMULATED   generated by us for the demo; never presented as a measurement
  NOT YET     cited as the next step, not connected in this build


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. LIVE DATA AND APIs
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. OpenWeatherMap (OpenWeather Ltd) - https://openweathermap.org  - LIVE
   What:  current weather and 5-day forecast for the farm's exact GPS point; map tiles for clouds, rain, wind.
   Why:   spraying a biological in wind or before rain wastes money, and heat stress changes the expected lift.
   Where: Today (live scene, spray window, rain tomorrow), Weather tab, temperature input to the yield model.

2. ANNAM.AI MCII sensor stations (ANNAM.AI) - https://annam.ai  - LIVE (cached copy as fallback)
   What:  real field-station readings: soil moisture, soil temperature, solar radiation, leaf wetness.
   Why:   hyperlocal ground truth that a weather API cannot give; feeds the "readiness to spray" score.
   Where: Weather tab station map, readiness score on Today.

3. Google Gemini (Google) - https://ai.google.dev  - LIVE
   What:  AI assistant (text, voice, photo), farm-waste photo check, machine translation of the interface.
   Why:   farmers ask in their own words and languages; Gemini answers in plain language grounded in the farm's numbers.
   Where: AI Chat tab, Farm Waste Market photo check, all 8 Indian languages.
   Note:  never used in the ROI maths. Falls back across models when one is out of free quota.

4. Supabase (PostgreSQL cloud database) - https://supabase.com  - LIVE (local file fallback)
   What:  stores the Season Journal, telemetry snapshots, company needs, offers, waste listings, buyers, river incidents.
   Why:   data must survive between sessions and be shared between farmers, companies and officers.
   Where: Management (Season Journal), Company Needs, Farm Waste Market, River Watch console.

5. OpenStreetMap Nominatim (OSM Foundation) - https://nominatim.org  - LIVE
   What:  turns GPS into a village/town name and lets the farmer search a place.
   Why:   "Phaltan, Satara" means more to a farmer than 17.99, 74.43; also picks the agro-climatic belt.
   Where: location bar, "Search your village / town".

6. Browser GPS + ipwhois.app / ipapi.co - https://ipwhois.app , https://ipapi.co  - LIVE (fallback only)
   What:  the device's GPS first; approximate network location only if GPS is refused.
   Why:   the whole app (weather, soil belt, mandi, buyers, river) follows the farm's location automatically.
   Where: Live Location button and auto-locate on first visit. HTTPS only.

7. RainViewer - https://www.rainviewer.com  - LIVE
   What:  live rain radar layer.
   Why:   shows rain moving towards the field before spraying.
   Where: interactive weather map.

8. Map tiles: OpenStreetMap, Esri World Imagery, Leaflet - https://www.openstreetmap.org , https://leafletjs.com  - LIVE
   What:  base map, satellite view, map library.
   Why:   farmers recognise their field on a satellite view.
   Where: weather map, station map, company and buyer maps.

B. GOVERNMENT AND RESEARCH REFERENCE DATA
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

9. Agmarknet (Directorate of Marketing & Inspection, Govt of India) - https://agmarknet.gov.in  - SNAPSHOT
   What:  mandi (APMC) modal prices and arrivals for 24 commodities (report of 4-6 Sep 2026, bundled file).
   Why:   ROI is lift x price; a farmer needs the price his crop actually sells for, and whether it is above MSP.
   Where: Field tab commodity cards, mandi panel, ROI (plain price only, no assumed premium), Company Needs price check.

10. MSP 2026-27 (CACP / Ministry of Agriculture) - https://cacp.dacnet.nic.in  - SNAPSHOT
    What:  Minimum Support Price per crop.
    Why:   tells the farmer if the mandi is paying below the government floor (then register for MSP procurement).
    Where: mandi panel, pricing engine.

11. Soil Health Card scheme (DA&FW, Govt of India) - https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in  - REFERENCE
    What:  12-parameter soil standards (N, P, K, OC, pH, EC, S, Zn, Fe, Cu, Mn, B) and regional High/Medium/Low shares.
    Why:   biologicals respond to soil pH and organic carbon; nutrient gaps decide the fertiliser plan.
    Where: Soil tab (donut cards), fertiliser plan, yield model inputs.
    Note:  regional reference values, not the farmer's own soil test.

12. ICAR institutes - https://icar.org.in  (IISR soybean, IIWBR wheat, NRRI rice, CICR cotton, IIMR maize, SBI sugarcane,
    IIHR horticulture, DOGR onion & garlic)  - REFERENCE
    What:  recommended N-P-K doses, crop stages, yield ceilings per crop.
    Why:   the fertiliser and "best realistic practice" scenarios must follow official recommendations.
    Where: Impact & ROI scenarios, Management, crop profiles.

13. TNAU Agritech portal (Tamil Nadu Agricultural University) - https://agritech.tnau.ac.in  - SNAPSHOT
    What:  crop catalogue, diseases and pests, with symptoms and management.
    Why:   gives the leaf scanner and the assistant trusted, Indian disease and pest knowledge.
    Where: LeafVision results, Biologicals tab, AI Chat.

14. IMD (India Meteorological Department) - https://mausam.imd.gov.in  - NOT YET (cited)
    Why:   official rainfall normals to replace the season normals the model uses today.

15. Sentinel-2 NDVI (ESA Copernicus) - https://dataspace.copernicus.eu  - NOT YET
    Why:   real crop vigour from satellite; today NDVI uses a labelled season normal.

16. SoilGrids (ISRIC) - https://soilgrids.org  - NOT YET
    Why:   field-level soil estimates where no soil test exists.

D. OUR OWN DATA AND MODELS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

21. Simulated field trials (this team) - data/field_trials.csv  - SIMULATED
    What:  1,600 paired plots (treated + untreated), 12 crops, 8 regions, 2021-2025, generated with a fixed seed.
    Why:   no open, plot-level Indian trial data with treated/untreated pairs exists; simulation lets us build and
           test the full method (prediction, attribution, ROI) end to end.
    Where: trains the yield model. Every screen says the data is simulated.

22. Yield model: Random Forest + TreeSHAP (this team) - models/  - SIMULATED training
    What:  predicts yield (q/acre); SHAP explains the prediction; with/without counterfactual estimates the lift.
    Why:   answers "did the product or the rain do it?" with numbers, and shows its own accuracy per crop.
    Where: Yield & Attributes, Impact & ROI, Today.
    Honest numbers: within-crop R2 -0.27 to 0.69; typical error 3-13% of the crop's mean (pipeline/evaluate_model.py).

23. Season normals (this team) - models/season_normals.json  - SIMULATED
    What:  per-crop typical rainfall, GDD and NDVI from the training data.
    Why:   keeps the model inside what it learned instead of fixed numbers for every crop; labelled "not a live reading".

24. Demo companies, buyers and river readings (this team)  - SIMULATED, marked DEMO
    Why:   shows how Company Needs, Farm Waste Market and River Watch work before real partners join.
    Note:  fictional names only; no real company has posted.

25. AquaSentinel R3 firmware thresholds (this team's river float design)  - REFERENCE
    What:  pH outside 6-9, dissolved oxygen below 4 mg/L, conductivity above 2,250 uS/cm.
    Why:   the same limits the float will use, so app alerts match the hardware. The float is designed, not built.
